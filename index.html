The squishiest game of hide-and-seek ever bean made.
How to play
  • Each round a spotlight roulette picks one Seeker. Everyone else hides!
  • Hiders: 25s to hide while the seeker counts. Crouch (C) inside bushes & boxes or under tables/beds to be nearly invisible. Q Disguise as a prop, F Taunt for bonus points (but it reveals you!). Earn points every second you survive.
  • Seeker: find and tag everyone (E / click when close, or just bump them). Q Sniff points to the nearest hider, F Dash.
  • WASD move · mouse look · Space jump · Shift sprint · M mute · Esc pause. Caught? Fly around as a ghost — hold T to fast-forward.
  • Try the trampoline, the slide (climb the ladder!) and the pond. 🦆
