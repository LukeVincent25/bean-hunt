The squishiest game of hide-and-seek ever bean made.
How to play
  • Each round a spotlight roulette picks one Seeker. Everyone else hides!
  • Hiders: hide while the seeker counts (longer on the big city map). Crouch (C) inside bushes & boxes or under tables/beds to be nearly invisible. Q Disguise as a prop, F Taunt for bonus points (but it reveals you!). Earn points every second you survive.
  • Seeker: find and tag everyone (E when close, or just bump them). Q Sniff points to the nearest hider, F Dash.
  • WASD move · mouse look · Space jump · Shift sprint · M mute · Esc pause. Caught? Fly around as a ghost — hold T to fast-forward.
  • 🪝 ODM grappling gear (every bean!): aim with the reticle — it glows when a wall is in range (60 m). Hold Left click / Right click to fire & hang on to the left / right hook (or hold G), let go to fly off with all your momentum. Space while hooked = gas-boost & reel in, Shift in mid-air = gas thrust. Gas refills while you're on the ground.
  • Walled City: a huge AoT-style town (~2× bigger): 8–12 floor towers, a 46 m wall, bridges, a raised castle district and a taller clock tower. Hide high! (Bots grapple too.) Backyard: a bit roomier — trampoline, slide and pond. 🦆
  • 👹 Giant beans: lumbering titans roam the map. If one grabs you, mash Space to wriggle free (~4s) or get yeeted across town (and briefly revealed if you were hiding). Slice a giant's nape (back of the neck) while zipping past on your hooks at high speed to stun it for ~10s and score big. Grapples stick to giants too. Toggle giants in the menu / lobby.
